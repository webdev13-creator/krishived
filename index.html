THE CHALLENGE

One farm is not
one uniform field.

Soil moisture, crop vigour, and nutrient needs can vary within a single plot. Blanket decisions can miss the zones that need attention most.

⌁

Signals get missed

Small changes in crop vigour and moisture are difficult to notice when the whole field is evaluated as a single unit.

01
◌

Inputs can be mistimed

Weather fluctuations, crop stage, and moisture deficits all influence whether irrigation or fertilizer will help or harm.

02
⌖

Advice needs validation

Satellite maps provide spatial clues; targeted soil testing and agronomy are essential to confirm actual nutrient requirements.

03
THE SOLUTION · PRECISION AGRICULTURE

From space data to
smarter decisions.

KrishiVed combines satellite observations, weather context, and AI pattern detection with local soil testing and agronomy—delivering clear guidance in the farmer's language.

◎

Right input, right place

Focus fertilizer and scouting only where needed instead of blanket broadcast applications across the entire farm.

◌

Smarter water use

Prioritise irrigation timing using moisture patterns and rainfall forecasts, avoiding water stress and root waterlogging.

!

Earlier crop-stress alerts

Spot unusual canopy patterns 5–7 days sooner than naked eye scouting to inspect problems before yield loss occurs.

₹

Lower unnecessary spending

Avoid over-spending on ineffective chemical inputs by confirming true soil nutrient needs through lab validation.

▤

Better field records

Track crop development and compare zone health across multiple seasons to build institutional field knowledge.

🗣

Local-language access

Share simple, actionable advisories through mobile app, SMS or voice in Hindi, Urdu, and regional languages.
